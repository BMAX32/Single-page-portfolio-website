Autonomous Car

A small self-driving car built around an Arduino. It detects obstacles and track markings with its sensors, streams live video through a camera module, and steers itself by controlling the motors through a driver board.

Components

  • Arduino
  • ESP32-CAM
  • Ultrasonic sensor
  • Infrared sensor
  • Motor driver

How it works

  • The ultrasonic sensor measures the distance ahead so the car can stop or turn before hitting an obstacle.
  • The infrared sensor detects the line or edge of the surface under the car.
  • The ESP32-CAM provides the camera feed.
  • The Arduino reads the sensors, decides what to do, and sends commands to the motor driver, which powers the wheels.
